> **Key Takeaways**
>
> - The national median cost of assisted living is **$6,200 per month**, or **$74,400 per year** (2025). That is up 5% from 2024.
> - Your state matters most. The median runs from **$4,369 per month** in Mississippi to **$12,096** in Hawaii (2025), about 2.8 times higher (our calculation).
> - Prices rose **21.6%** between 2022 and 2025.
> - The monthly fee usually covers a room, meals, 24/7 staff and activities. Higher levels of care, a one-time community fee and yearly rent increases can add to the bill.
> - Medicare does not pay for assisted living. Medicaid may pay for care services in some states, but not for room and board.

---

You are probably looking at a price sheet from an assisted living community, or planning ahead, and wondering whether the number is normal. How much does assisted living cost per month? The honest answer is "it depends on where you live and how much help is needed." A fair yardstick makes that conversation easier.

This article gives you the national and state medians from the most recent CareScout Cost of Care Survey, which covers 2025. You will see what the monthly fee usually includes and what can cost extra, how prices have changed, what a longer stay adds up to, and how assisted living compares with other types of care. You will also see where help with paying may come from. Your own price may differ, so use these figures as a starting point and ask each community for a written fee schedule.

## How Much Does Assisted Living Cost Per Month? The National Median

The 2025 CareScout Cost of Care Survey reports the following national median for assisted living. A median means half of the prices were higher and half were lower.

| Period | National median (2025) |
| ------ | ---------------------- |
| **Per month** | $6,200 |
| **Per year** | $74,400 |
| **Per day** | about $204 |

*The daily figure is our calculation: $74,400 divided by 365 days.*

The median rose **5%** from 2024. The annual figure equals the monthly fee multiplied by 12.

*Note: CareScout collected prices from July to November 2025. About 4,944 licensed assisted living communities answered the survey. In California, assisted living is called a residential care facility, and the survey counts it. The prices are as reported by providers for a one-bedroom unit.*

## How Assisted Living Prices Have Changed

Prices rose in each of the last two surveys. The national median was **$70,800 per year** in 2024, a **10%** increase over 2023. It rose another 5% to **$74,400** in 2025. In all, CareScout reports that assisted living prices rose **21.6%** from 2022 to 2025.

Increases are not even across the country. In that analysis, North Dakota was the only state where the assisted living median fell, by 8%, while Hawaii's rose 36.9%. If you are budgeting for several years, do not assume today's price will hold. Ask each community how often it raises rent and by how much.

## What the Monthly Fee Usually Covers, and What Costs Extra

Assisted living is usually billed as one monthly fee. According to CareScout, the baseline services are a room, meals, WiFi, 24/7 staff and on-site activities, and residents typically also get housekeeping and help with daily activities. What a community includes in the base price is its own choice, so ask.

CareScout names these things that can raise the price:

- **Level of care.** Costs rise with the amount of help a resident needs.
- **Location, apartment size and amenities.** A larger unit or a premium community costs more.
- **A one-time community fee.** This is paid when you move in.
- **Annual rent increases.** The monthly rate usually goes up from year to year.
- **Memory care.** Specialized care for dementia is priced separately.

The survey does not say how much each of these items adds, so do not assume a number. Ask for the exact amounts in writing.

## What Assisted Living Costs Over Time

Families often ask what a whole stay will cost. A monthly median makes it easy to estimate. These are our calculations, not survey results, and they assume the price stays at the 2025 median:

| Length of stay | Cost at the national median |
| -------------- | --------------------------- |
| **6 months** | $37,200 |
| **1 year** | $74,400 |
| **2 years** | $148,800 |
| **3 years** | $223,200 |

Real prices usually rise from year to year, and care needs can change. Use the table as a floor, not a forecast.

## Assisted Living Cost Per Month vs Other Types of Care

Among the options in the survey, assisted living sits in the middle. Here are the 2025 national medians side by side:

| Type of care | Monthly cost (2025) |
| ------------ | ------------------- |
| **Adult day health care, 5 days a week** | $2,058 |
| **Assisted living** | $6,200 |
| **In-home care, 44 hours a week** | $6,673 |
| **Nursing home, semi-private room** | $9,581 |
| **Nursing home, private room** | $10,798 |

The services are not the same. Assisted living bundles housing, meals and help with daily tasks. In-home care is billed by the hour and covers only the caregiver's time, so you also pay for housing and other costs. CareScout suggests that needing more than 44 hours of help a week may be a signal to compare the cost of assisted living. For the home care side, see [how much in-home care costs per month](/article/in-home-care/how-much-does-in-home-care-cost-per-month/).

## Who Helps Pay for Assisted Living

Without another payer, the resident or the family pays the monthly fee. Here is what each program does and does not cover:

- **Medicare.** Medicare does not pay for long-term care, including care in an assisted living facility. Our article [Does Medicare Cover Assisted Living?](/article/assisted-living/does-medicare-cover-assisted-living/) explains what it does pay for.
- **Medicaid.** Medicaid may cover long-term care if you meet your state's rules. Federal rules generally do not allow its home and community-based waivers to pay for room and board, which means rent and meals stay with the resident.
- **VA Aid and Attendance.** Veterans and surviving spouses who qualify for a VA pension can receive an increased amount for help with daily activities. The 2026 maximum is **$29,093 per year** for a veteran with no dependents, about **$2,424 per month**, and **$18,697 per year** for a surviving spouse, about **$1,558 per month**. The monthly amounts are our division of VA's annual rates. The actual payment depends on income.
- **Long-term care insurance.** If a policy exists, check whether it covers assisted living. Our article on [whether long-term care insurance is worth it](/article/senior-care-costs/long-term-care-insurance-worth-it/) explains how benefits work.

For the full picture, see our [complete guide to assisted living](/article/assisted-living/assisted-living-complete-guide/) and [What Is Assisted Living?](/article/assisted-living/what-is-assisted-living/)

## Questions to Ask Before You Sign

The same monthly fee can cover different things at different communities. Ask each one:

1. What exactly does the monthly fee include?
2. How is the level of care priced, and what happens if needs increase?
3. What is the one-time community fee, and is any of it refundable?
4. How much notice do you give before raising the rent, and how much did you raise it last year?
5. Will you put the rate and all fees in writing?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does assisted living cost per year?

The 2025 national median is **$74,400 per year**, up 5% from 2024. State medians range from about $52,425 in Mississippi to $145,155 in Hawaii.

### What is the average cost of assisted living per day?

At the 2025 national median of $6,200 per month, assisted living works out to about **$204 per day** (our calculation). Communities bill monthly, so the daily figure is only a way to compare.

### Which state has the cheapest assisted living?

In the 2025 survey, Mississippi has the lowest median at **$4,369 per month**, followed by Alabama at $4,425. Hawaii has the highest at **$12,096 per month**, followed by Alaska at $9,882.

### Is assisted living cheaper than a nursing home?

Yes, at the national median. Assisted living costs **$6,200 per month** (2025), compared with $9,581 for a semi-private nursing home room and $10,798 for a private one. The care is different, because nursing homes provide skilled nursing care.

### Does Medicare pay for assisted living?

No. Medicare does not pay for long-term care, including care in an assisted living facility. Our article [Does Medicare Cover Assisted Living?](/article/assisted-living/does-medicare-cover-assisted-living/) explains what it does pay for.
