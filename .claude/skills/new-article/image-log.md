# Image log

Every article image on the site, so a new one is never equal or close to an existing one. Add a row
when an article is published (step 9 of the publish phase in `SKILL.md`).

## Overused motifs (avoid unless the article really needs them)

- **Brunette woman in her 40s-50s + white-haired parent**: appears in 5 of 7 images.
- **Wooden kitchen table** with mugs, plants and a bright window on the left: 3 of 7.
- **Reviewing papers/bills** at a table: 2 of 7.
- **Cardigans in cream, green or navy**, soft golden morning light, potted plants: almost all.
- Holding hands as the main gesture: 3 of 7.

New prompts should change at least three of: **setting**, **people** (age, sex, ethnicity, number,
who is the caregiver), **action**, **framing** (wide, close-up, over-the-shoulder, detail), **time
of day / palette**, **style** (photo vs. illustration).
